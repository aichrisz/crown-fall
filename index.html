Rules and controls

    
    
      
  1. The board is 15×15. Every cell holds 0–3 grains and belongs to you, the rival, or nobody.
    2. 
      
  2. On your turn place one grain on a cell you own, or on a neutral cell orthogonally touching your territory.
        You can never place directly on enemy ground.
    3. 
      
  3. Placing on neutral ground claims it. A cell holding 4 grains topples: it loses 4 and sends one grain to each
        orthogonal neighbour. Grains pushed off the edge are gone.
    4. 
      
  4. Every in-bounds cell an avalanche touches becomes yours — that is how territory and crowns change hands. The
        entire cascade belongs to the side that triggered it.
    5. 
      
  5. Five crown sites are seeded per board, mirrored through 180° so both sides face the same layout. Hold three
        crowns at the end of your turn to win — but no crown win registers until both sides have taken three turns.
    6. 
      
  6. If neither side can move, or at the 160-turn cap, the match is ranked on crowns, then territory, then grains,
        then declared a draw.
    7. 
      
  7. READ: three charges per side per match. It previews the exact avalanche and capture footprint of the focused
        cell without changing anything. The heuristic rival simulates freely — it costs it nothing.
    8. 
      
  8. The rival is a plain heuristic, not a deep engine: it scores every legal placement one move ahead with a single
        simulation and takes the best. It does no search and never models your reply, so it can be baited — leave a
        loaded cell where its capture hands you a bigger avalanche in return.
    9. 
      
  9. Rival doctrine picks what that one-ply heuristic values: Warden balances crowns, captures and ground; Reaper
        hunts captures and enemy ground and ignores grains lost over the edge; Surveyor spreads quietly, hoards grains
        and creeps toward unheld crowns. All three are deterministic and none of them searches — the same position
        always draws the same move. Choosing one starts a new match, and it is disabled in hotseat.
    10. 
      
  10. Keys: arrows move, Home/End jump along a row, Enter or Space places, R reads, N new game, P pause, H help,
        Escape closes help.
